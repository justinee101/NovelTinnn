Synopsis: A reader finds the web novel he followed has become reality.

Chapter 1: The Story Begins

Kim Dokja checks his favorite webnovel, Three Ways to Survive in a Ruined World, only to find that it has come to an end after 10 years, with 3,149 chapters and an upcoming epilogue. He reminisces on his life during the time he was reading Ways of Survival. He then writes a comment thanking the author tls123 for the novel, and posts on the Recommendation Board of the webnovel site, recommending Ways of Survival. His post immediately garners comments pointing out that he has previously recommended the same novel, and that Ways of Survival is banned on the board. His recommendation is reported shortly after. Soon after, Kim Dokja receives a message from tls123 thanking him for his patronage of Ways of Survival, and telling him that the novel has won an unknown competition. The author asks for Kim Dokja's email address and promises to send a special gift to him as thanks for his help. They inform him that Ways of Survival will shortly become monetized, and that the epilogue must be paid for. Kim Dokja agrees to pay for it, and tls123 does not send any more messages. Kim Dokja is somewhat disappointed at the sudden end of the conversation, and idly wonders what the gift will be. His narration then comments that he had no idea what was going to happen to the world the next day.

This is a short overview of the opening in my own words. Read the official release to enjoy the full chapter.
