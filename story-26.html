Slice of Life Author: Satsuki Yoshino

Synopsis: A calligrapher exiled to a remote island learns from its villagers.

Chapter 1: The Story Begins

The story begins with the core conflict: Seishuu Handa punches an elderly art curator who criticized his gallery submission as unoriginal and "textbook-like". Shamed by his own violent outburst, Handa's father unceremoniously exiles the high-strung, 23-year-old city boy to a remote village on the Gotō Islands. Handa views this retreat as a harsh punishment and a quiet isolation zone where he can sullenly grind away at his calligraphy to reclaim his reputation. Handa arrives at his destination on the back of an old farmer's tractor. He immediately experiences overwhelming big-city culture shock. When Handa tries to clean up his new home, he catches a hyperactive 7-year-old local village girl named Naru Kotoishi sneaking around inside. Because the house had been sitting empty, Naru had taken it over as her personal playground and secret hideout. Handa physically throws Naru out of the house so he can work, but she possesses zero boundaries. She effortlessly climbs back through the open windows, completely intrigued by the strange city boy and his calligraphy brushes.
