Our Mission

NovelTin is a webnovel-style reading site made for ITE 1. It lists 30 popular manga, light novels and web novels, each with an author, genre, synopsis and a short chapter overview. It has a dark/light theme, likes, a reading progress bar and a searchable genre filter.
