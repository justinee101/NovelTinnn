Chapter 1: The Story Begins

In the 74th Floor Labyrinth, Kirito had a battle with a Level 82 «Lizardman Lord». After defeating the monster with his four-hit Sword Skill, Horizontal Square, he recollected what occurred two years ago. Klein asked for Kirito's guidance after presuming that the latter was among the one thousand participants of the «Sword Art Online Closed Beta». After Klein successfully defeated a «Frenzy Boar», he tried to log out because he ordered pizza. Failing to find the logout button, Kirito and Klein discussed the implications of the "unable to logout bug".
