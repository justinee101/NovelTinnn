Synopsis: A student finds a notebook that kills anyone whose name is written in it.

Chapter 1: The Story Begins

The Shinigami Realm is the home of the Shinigami, demon-like creatures whose world is a barren, rocky, and desolate area. One such Shinigami is Ryuk, who is watching a couple of fellow Shinigami play with skulls as a form of gambling similar to dice. He tells them that he is off to look for his Death Note, which he mislaid some time ago. The Shinigami King has given him a new Death Note, but now he is going to look for the other one. Ryuk enters a trans-dimensional portal and arrives in the Human World. In the Human World is Light Yagami, a bored 17-year-old Japanese student. One day at school, he is staring out of a classroom window when he sees a black notebook suddenly appear as if by magic in the school yard.
