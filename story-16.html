Synopsis: The world's weakest hunter gains the power to level up without limit.

Chapter 1: The Story Begins

The chapter opens with Sung Jin‑Woo introducing himself while panting heavily, already injured before anything has even begun. He explains that he is an E‑Rank hunter, the absolute lowest rank, and that he never imagined his life would turn out this way. He reflects on how weak he is, how often he gets hurt, and how embarrassing it feels to even call himself a hunter. Despite this, he continues working because his mother is hospitalized and he needs the money to pay for her medical bills. His regeneration ability is barely above average, and everything else about him is painfully mediocre. The scene shifts to a construction site in Seoul where hunters are gathering for a raid. Jin‑Woo receives his daily allowance from a guild employee and thanks him. He then runs into an old acquaintance, Bae Yoon‑Seok, who had previously quit being a hunter but returned because his wife is pregnant with their second child. They talk about how dangerous the job is but how raids offer a chance to earn a lot of money quickly. Bae admits he’s nervous because he’s out of practice, and Jin‑Woo reassures him while also acknowledging internally that he himself is even weaker than before. As more hunters gather, Kim Sang‑Shik greets Jin‑Woo warmly and expresses concern for him. Jin‑Woo brushes it off, insisting he already ate and that Sang‑Shik doesn’t need to worry so much. Nearby, Bae asks about Jin‑Woo, wondering if he’s a strong hunter since everyone seems to treat him kindly. Another hunter explains that Jin‑Woo is actually known as “the world’s weakest hunter,” weaker than anyone else in the guild. They talk openly about how he even gets injured in E‑Rank dungeons and how his presence lowers the dungeon’s difficulty. They stop talking when they realize Jin‑Woo overheard them, though he pretends not to care and simply tries to order a cup of coffee. Unfortunately, the staff member tells him they just ran out, adding another small disappointment to his day. Ju‑Hee, the healer, arrives and immediately panics when she sees Jin‑Woo’s face injury. She scolds him gently, asking how he managed to hurt himself again. Jin‑Woo tries to laugh it off, saying it “just happened” during a hunt. Ju‑Hee presses him for the truth, and he eventually admits he ended up in the hospital after his last raid because his party didn’t bring a healer—they assumed they wouldn’t need one since everyone except him was higher ranked. Ju‑Hee is shocked, but Jin‑Woo insists he’s used to things like that happening. Mr. Song Chi‑Yul, the highest‑ranked hunter in the group, calls everyone together as the raid is about to begin. He asks if he can act as the party leader, and the entire group agrees without hesitation. They respect him and trust his experience. Once leadership is settled, the team prepares to enter the dungeon. Kim Sang‑Shik reminds Jin‑Woo not to get hurt or fall behind, and Jin‑Woo jokingly agrees. Ju‑Hee tells him it’s time to go, and he steels himself, determined to give everything he has despite knowing how weak he is. The chapter ends with the group stepping into the dungeon under Mr. Song’s command, Jin‑Woo following behind the others with a mix of anxiety and resolve, ready to face whatever awaits them even though he knows he’s the weakest one there.
