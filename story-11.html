Synopsis: A surgeon saves a boy's life and later must hunt what he became.

Chapter 1: The Story Begins

Another brilliant operation was performed on opera singer Rosenbach by Japanese Dr. Tenma and his colleagues in Eisler Memorial Hospital in Dusseldorf. News appear on TV about the current stable condition of Rosenbach and press conference held by Udo Heinemann (Tenma's director) and the news of the Liebert family who fled from East Germany to Dusseldorf. Michael Liebert is a foreign trade advisor. While having dinner with Eva Heinemann, Tenma recalls being blamed by a wife of the Turkish man for not performing an operation on her husband who arrived before Rosenbach and was supposed to be operated by Tenma but his operation was given to less skilled Dr. Becker, and as a result of that the Turkish man died due to Becker's sloppiness. Later that evening, a police report outbreaks about the murder of Liebert couple in their house. A boy is fatally wounded in head and the twin sister is found in state of shock, standing near her collapsed brother.
